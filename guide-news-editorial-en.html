Morning Routine: Update Course Status in 60 Seconds

Every morning, golfers check our website first to see whether carts are allowed and which greens are in play. The status box is located at the very top of the admin page.

⛳ SECTION 1: LIVE RESORT & COURSE STATUS (HOMEPAGE & DISPLAYS) ● LIVE SYNCHRONIZED
1. OPENING HOURS
09:00 - 15:00 hrs
Reception & Phones
2. GOLF CARTS
✓ ALLOWED
Note: stay on cart paths
3. TROLLEYS
✓ ALLOWED
Winter wheels recommended
4. COURSE & GREENS
✓ OPEN
18-Holes Regular

4-Step Quick Instructions:

1
Verify Reception & Phone Hours
Enter today’s service hours, e.g. daily 09:00 - 15:00 hrs. This dynamically updates guest guidance and phone assistant greetings.
2
Set Golf Carts & Trolleys Rules
Select state: Allowed (Green), Restricted (Amber), or Prohibited (Red).
Optional note: e.g. “Stay on cart paths only” or “Winter wheels required”.
3
Select Course Status & Greens (Use 1-Click Presets!)
Select whether the course is Open, Partially Open, or Closed (Frost/Weather), and whether Summer Greens or Winter Greens are in play.
Speed Tip: The „Schnellvorlage wählen“ dropdown automatically configures common scenarios with a single click (e.g. „18-Loch regulär geöffnet“ or „Witterungsbedingt gesperrt“).
4
Save & Publish (The Golden Button)
Click „Status jetzt speichern & veröffentlichen“ at the bottom of the card.
A green confirmation toast appears instantly. The public website and clubhouse TV screens update without reloading.
⚡ Instant Live Synchronization
Saved statuses broadcast immediately to web visitors and active digital clubhouse boards. No server reboot or manual deployment required.

Gut Wissmannshof Module 2: Content Studio
Publishing News & Tournaments

News Studio: Create, Format & Publish Articles

Publish engaging tournament reports, restaurant culinary specials, or course renovation updates with crisp photography and real-time live preview.

Key Input Fields:

1
Headline & Category
Catchy title (e.g. „Thrilling Finish at the 2026 Club Championships“).
Pick category: Turniere (Tournaments) Aktuelles (News) Gastronomie Platz & Resort.
2
Teaser (Card Summary)
2 to 3 punchy sentences (100–160 chars). This text is shown directly on homepage cards to entice readers to click.
3
Hero Featured Image
Click „Datei auswählen“ to upload a photo or pick from the Resort Image Library. The tool offers an automatic 16:9 aspect crop for clean card appearance.
4
Text Formatting Toolbar
Format your story using quick toolbar buttons:
+ Paragraph + Subheading (H3) + Quote Bold / Italic
📸

Embed Photos in Article Body

Click „+ Foto in Text einfügen“:

  • Upload multiple event or action shots.
  • Add a descriptive caption (e.g. „Award ceremony Ladies Gross“).
  • Website visitors can tap photos to view in full-screen 4K lightbox!
Mobile-optimized automatically
⏱

Time Scheduling & Expiry

Optionally set an expiry date (e.g. tournament signup deadline). Once expired, the article automatically moves to the archive.

👁 Real-Time Live Preview

The right column dynamically displays both: 1. Card Teaser Preview (homepage grid) and 2. Full Article View (reader layout).

Publishing: Click „Beitrag speichern & veröffentlichen“ to go live immediately. Choose status „Entwurf“ (Draft) if work is still in progress.

Management & Checklist

Article Management, Multi-Device Sync & Checklist

Below the editor, the central article table allows editing, changing visibility status, deleting, and downloading safety backups.

✏️ EDIT ARTICLE
Click the blue pencil icon to load the full post back into the editor for corrections.
🔄 1-CLICK STATUS
Toggle between Published (Green), Draft (Amber), and Archived (Gold) directly in the table.
🗑 DELETE ARTICLE
Click the red trash bin. A confirmation modal prevents accidental deletions.
☁️ Multi-Device Cloud Sync

Whether working at the front desk or on a laptop in the office, the green sync status confirms all workstations are in harmony.

💾 1-Click Backup Export

Click „JSON-Backup herunterladen“ to export an offline archive file of all articles and images to your local drive.
